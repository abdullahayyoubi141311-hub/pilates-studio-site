About Us

A calmer way to get strong

🤍

Tucked away in Al Rahba, Studio Noor was built around one idea: Pilates should feel personal. With only six reformers in the room, every class stays small and every instructor gives you real attention — correcting your form, adjusting your springs, and helping you move better with every session.

  • Small group classes, max 6 people
  • Certified reformer Pilates instructors
  • Beginner friendly, no experience needed
  • Calm, private studio atmosphere

                                        Recovery

                                        After Your Class

                                        What to drink once you're off the reformer, from the simple default to the situational upgrades.

                                        💧

                                        Water — the default

                                        Around 400–500 ml over the hour after class.

                                        🍋

                                        Water with salt and lemon, or an electrolyte tablet

                                        Worth it in Abu Dhabi summer, or if you sweat heavily or have a long walk to the car park.

                                        🥛

                                        Milk or a protein shake

                                        Useful if the session was strength-focused and your next meal is more than a couple of hours away. Roughly 20–25 g protein.

                                        🍶

                                        Laban or ayran

                                        Protein and salt together, and easy to find locally.

                                        ☕

                                        Green tea or black coffee

                                        Fine, just count them alongside your water rather than instead of it.
